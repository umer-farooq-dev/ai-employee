How prices are chosen
  1. The visitor's country, found automatically (Cloudflare). Preview another country: add ?region=US to the website address.
  2. The plan's own price in that currency (Plans → Prices in other currencies).
  3. Else the base price × the rate, rounded to a clean amount (36, 9,900, 128,000).
  4. Customers in your country pay GST; customers abroad get an export invoice without GST.
