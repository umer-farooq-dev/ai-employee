# User Guide — Company Panel

For businesses that use the AI Employee platform: owners, managers and human agents. The panel works in any browser on
a computer, tablet or phone, and can be installed as an app from the browser menu ("Install app" / "Add to Home
Screen") for notifications on the phone.

## Who sees what

| Role | Can do |
|---|---|
| **Owner / Admin** | Everything: AI Employees, settings, team, billing, data export and account deletion |
| **Manager** | Leads, calendar, calls, inbox, campaigns, knowledge, catalogue, analytics; no billing or account deletion |
| **Human Agent** | Their leads and follow-ups, inbox conversations handed to them, calendar; personal dashboard only |

The menu only shows what your role and your plan include.

## 1. Getting started

1. **Register** on the website (company name, industry, your details). After e-mail verification your company waits
   for approval by the platform team; you see a status page until then.
2. After approval your **ready-made AI Employee** for your industry is created. The **Dashboard** shows a getting-started
   checklist: company details, AI Employee, knowledge, catalogue, phone number, test, activation.
3. Fill **Settings → Company** (business hours, address, GST details for invoices).
4. Invite your team: **Team → Invite** (e-mail and role).

## 2. AI Employees

**AI Employees** → open one to change it step by step:
- **Identity**: name, voice, languages, greeting, personality.
- **Knowledge & catalogue**: which knowledge and products it may use.
- **Tools**: what it may do — book appointments, create follow-ups, send WhatsApp, hand over to a person.
- **Rules**: when to hand over (upset customer, asks for a person), working hours, what never to say.
- **Test**: chat with it, make a **browser test call**, or a **test call to your phone**. Fix answers, then test again.
- **Activate** when ready; **Pause** stops it at once. The **Analytics** button shows this AI Employee's numbers.

The AI always says it is an AI assistant. It never invents prices or offers that are not in your knowledge or catalogue.

## 3. Leads (CRM)

- **Leads**: every enquiry from calls, WhatsApp, website chat, forms and imports, with a score (hot / warm / cold),
  status, owner and full history (calls with recording and summary, chats, notes, appointments).
- Filter, search, bulk-assign, change status; **Import** from Excel / CSV (duplicates are matched by phone / e-mail and
  reported); **Export**.
- **Follow-ups**: tasks with due dates; reminders arrive by e-mail and in the bell.
- **Settings → CRM**: your own fields, scoring rules and automatic assignment to team members.

## 4. Calendar

Appointments booked by the AI or your team (site visits, consultations, demos…) with reminders to the customer on
WhatsApp / e-mail. Double booking is prevented. Holidays and business hours are respected.

## 5. Calls

- **Calls**: every inbound and outbound call — recording, transcript, summary, score, follow-up.
- **Call now** from a lead, or **schedule** a call. Retries for busy / no answer follow your calling rules.
- **Settings → Calling & numbers**: your number, calling hours (within the legal limits set by the platform), retry
  attempts, recording.

## 6. Inbox (website chat and WhatsApp)

All live conversations in one place. The AI answers first; when it hands over (or you click **Take over**) a team member
replies, then **Hand back** to the AI. Agents get a notification for handovers, hot leads and bookings.
**Settings → WhatsApp**: your WhatsApp number and approved message templates. **AI Employees → Website chat** gives
the code to put the chat button on your website.

## 7. Campaigns

Outbound calling to a list of leads (with consent): choose the AI Employee, the leads, a short brief and the products to
offer, the calling window and pace. Starting a campaign needs an owner or manager. Results show dialled, connected,
qualified, hot and appointments.

## 8. Knowledge Base and Catalogue

- **Knowledge Base**: upload documents, add website pages or FAQs. Each source must be **approved** before the AI uses
  it. **Unanswered questions** lists what customers asked that the AI could not answer — add the answer as an FAQ.
- **Catalogue**: your properties / tours / courses / products with prices and details, grouped in collections; import
  from Excel.

## 9. Dashboard and Analytics

The **Dashboard** shows today / 7 days / 30 days / a custom range: AI Employees, calls, connected calls, qualified and
hot leads, appointments, conversions, human transfers and AI usage, with charts that refresh live.
**Analytics** compares AI Employees and shows each campaign's funnel.

## 10. Billing & Usage

- Your plan, renewal date and **usage this month** (what is left), AI credits, invoices (PDF, with GST) and payments.
- **Plans**: upgrade (you pay the difference now), downgrade (from the next renewal), monthly / yearly, cancel (stays
  active until the end of the paid period).
- Payment by card, UPI or net banking. If a renewal fails you get e-mails and some grace days; after that the AI
  Employees pause until payment — your data is kept.
- **Billing details**: GSTIN, legal name and address printed on invoices.

## 11. Support

**Support → New ticket**: category, priority, description and a screenshot. You get an e-mail and a bell notification
when the team replies. Answered tickets close automatically after some days without a reply.

## 12. Data & privacy (owner)

**Settings → Data & privacy**:
- **Export**: a ZIP of all your company's data and files; the download link is e-mailed and expires after a few days.
- **Delete account**: type the company name to confirm. Deletion happens after a grace period and can be cancelled
  until then. Invoices are kept as required by tax law.

## 13. Your profile and security

**My Profile**: name, phone, timezone, availability (online / away), password, **two-factor authentication** (strongly
recommended; your company may require it), active sessions (sign out other devices).

Need help? Raise a ticket from **Support**, or write to the support e-mail shown in the footer.
